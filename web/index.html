premise

Fluent is not the same as true. We stopped trusting confident answers. We started measuring them.

We do not ask the model whether it is right. We observe where its claims land on the knowledge of your papers.

EpistemeGraph turns every answer into a graph of claims and measures it against a knowledge graph built from your own PDFs: provenance, entailment, curvature, sheaf energy and spectra. A model trained on labelled answers decides, and a conformal layer bounds how often it is wrong.

  • we extract every claim
  • we trace it to a source sentence
  • we measure the geometry it bends
  • we learn the decision from labels
  • we bound the error rate
signal conditions

A hallucination rarely announces itself in the words. It shows up as tension in the geometry: claims that disagree on an overlap, edges that bend the graph, a spectrum that drifts. Move the pointer through each field.

The CorpusPHASE 1

PDFs become a provenance-verified knowledge graph. A triple enters only if its evidence quote is found in the source chunk. The graph's fidelity SKG is computed automatically and audited for precision with a confidence interval.

Knowledge-graph fidelity · intrinsic

—
Φprov precision proxy: mean evidence-grounding score of every extracted triple, rejected ones included.
Φcov recall proxy: how well the accepted triples cover the sentences of each chunk.
Φtopo connectivity: 1 for one connected graph, 0 for isolated facts.
Φcons consistency: share of triples that break no functional relation.

          006/ 007

          The Calibration BrainPHASE 4

          Ten model families, including a multiple-instance network that pinpoints the unsupported claim, compete under nested cross-validation on a proper scoring rule. The champion's probabilities are pooled with the local KG fidelity, and a conformal predictor turns them into decisions with a guaranteed error rate. Nothing is hand-tuned.
